Classic Pancakes 🥞

A quick and easy recipe for fluffy breakfast pancakes.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons of sugar
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 egg

Instructions :

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, milk, and egg in a bowl until smooth.
  2. Heat a lightly oiled frying pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour the batter onto the pan and flip when bubbles form.
  4. Serve hot with maple syrup or butter!
