CCOKIES RECIPE

Ingredients :

  • Flour : 2 cups
  • Sugar : 1 cup
  • Butter : 1 cup
  • Chocolate Chips : 1 cup

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix : Stir all ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Scoop : Place dough balls on a tray.
  3. Bake : Bake at 350°F for 10 minutes.
